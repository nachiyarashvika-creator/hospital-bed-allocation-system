How smart allocation works

Matches the patient's condition and ward gender, preserves critical-care resources, and serves waiting patients by severity. Severity 5 is highest. Compatible patients with equal severity are served in arrival order.

Find the right bed

Suggest a bed first, or admit directly. If no compatible bed is ready, the patient joins the waiting list.
